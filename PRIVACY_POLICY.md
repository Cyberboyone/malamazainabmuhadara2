# Privacy Policy

**Malama Zainab Jaafar Muhadara 2** (package: `com.nakudin.malamazainabmuhadara2`)
Effective date: 19 August 2026

This privacy policy describes how the app collects and handles information.

## Data collected

The app itself collects **no personal data**. All audio lessons are bundled
inside the app and playback is fully offline.

The app displays advertising banners served by **Google AdMob**. Google may
collect and process data (such as device identifiers, IP address and usage
data) to serve ads, in accordance with Google's privacy policy at
https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data when
you use apps at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The app stores playback progress locally on your device (SharedPreferences)
so you can resume listening where you left off. This data never leaves your
device.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — used only to load AdMob advertisements.
- **Media playback / foreground service** — used to keep audio playing in
  the background and show playback controls in the notification shade.

## Children's privacy

The app may be used by children. It does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us personal
information through the advertising SDKs, contact us and we will take steps
to remove such information.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above reflects the
latest revision.

## Contact

For any privacy questions, contact the developer via the email address
listed on the Google Play listing for this app.
